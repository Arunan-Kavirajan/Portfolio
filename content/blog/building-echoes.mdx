title: "Building Echoes: a starfield of anonymous messages"

excerpt: "How I built a canvas-based anonymous messaging platform with React, Supabase, and Framer Motion."
---

Echoes started as a simple idea: what if anonymous messages could feel less like a wall of text and more like something you discover, drifting past like stars.

The core of the site is a canvas starfield where each message becomes a floating card. Under the hood it's React and Vite on the frontend, Supabase for the backend and moderation, and Framer Motion driving the drift animation.

More posts on the specific technical challenges — rate limiting, the admin panel, and the monthly constellation view — coming soon.
